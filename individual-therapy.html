Individual Therapy

Individual therapy is one-to-one, confidential talk therapy focused entirely on you — your circumstances, goals, and pace — covering anything from a specific challenge to broader self-understanding.

Book a Consultation Read FAQs

Who individual therapy may be for

  • Anyone wanting dedicated, one-to-one support for a personal challenge
  • People navigating stress, transitions, or difficult decisions
  • Those who prefer a private setting over group or family formats
  • Anyone wanting to build self-understanding at their own pace
  • People new to therapy who want to start with one-to-one sessions

Common reasons people seek individual therapy

  • Ongoing stress, overwhelm, or burnout
  • Navigating a major life decision or transition
  • Wanting a confidential space focused entirely on your own experience
  • Working through a specific personal challenge privately
  • Building self-awareness and coping strategies over time

What sessions may involve

Sessions are one-to-one with a therapist, structured around your specific goals, and can be short-term and focused or longer-term and open-ended depending on what you're working through.

What to expect

The first session typically involves discussing your background and what brought you to therapy, so the therapist can suggest an appropriate way forward together with you.

Approaches used

Individual therapy can draw on a range of approaches — including CBT, psychodynamic, and integrative methods — matched to your goals and preferences; your therapist can explain their approach.

Possible goals

Individual therapy may help you manage specific challenges, build coping strategies, and develop greater self-understanding. Pace and outcomes are individual and not guaranteed.

Individual therapy may help you manage specific challenges, build coping strategies, and develop greater self-understanding. Results vary by person, and no specific outcome can be guaranteed.

When to consider individual therapy

Consider individual therapy if you want dedicated, private support for something specific, or simply want space to think things through with a trained professional.

Frequently asked questions

What is individual therapy?Toggle answer

Individual therapy is one-to-one talk therapy between you and a trained therapist, focused on your specific goals, challenges, and pace.

What happens in an individual therapy session?Toggle answer

Sessions typically involve open conversation guided by the therapist, exploring your current concerns and working toward goals you agree on together.

When should someone consider individual therapy?Toggle answer

Consider it when you want dedicated, confidential support for a specific challenge, a life transition, or general self-understanding.

How is individual therapy different from group therapy?Toggle answer

Individual therapy is private and one-to-one, while group therapy involves multiple participants and peer interaction — some people use both at different times.

How should I prepare for my first therapy session?Toggle answer

No specific preparation is required. It can help to think about what you'd like support with, but the therapist will guide the conversation.
