Divorce Counseling

Divorce counseling supports individuals or couples through the emotional and practical difficulty of separation or divorce, offering space to process the transition and communicate more effectively during a difficult time.

Book a Consultation Read FAQs

Who divorce counseling may be for

  • Couples considering separation who want support communicating clearly
  • Individuals processing the emotional impact of a divorce already underway
  • Co-parents wanting to communicate more effectively for their children's sake
  • Anyone navigating the uncertainty of a relationship ending
  • People wanting support rebuilding a sense of stability after separation

Common reasons people seek divorce counseling

  • Difficulty communicating during separation or divorce proceedings
  • Processing grief, anger, or relief connected to the end of a relationship
  • Co-parenting concerns during or after separation
  • Uncertainty about whether to separate and wanting a clearer perspective
  • Wanting support rebuilding routines and stability after divorce

What sessions may involve

Sessions may involve one or both partners, depending on the situation, and focus on communication, emotional processing, and practical coping strategies during the transition.

What to expect

The first session focuses on understanding where things currently stand and what kind of support would be most useful — whether that's individual processing, joint communication support, or both.

Approaches used

Divorce counseling draws on general counseling and family-systems frameworks, adapted to the specific needs of separation and its emotional and practical impact.

Possible goals

Divorce counseling may help reduce conflict during separation, support healthier communication (including co-parenting), and provide space to process the emotional impact of the transition.

When to consider divorce counseling

Consider divorce counseling if communication during separation has become difficult, if co-parenting conversations are breaking down, or if you want support processing the emotional side of the transition.

Frequently asked questions

What is divorce counseling?Toggle answer

Divorce counseling is talk therapy that supports individuals or couples through the emotional and practical challenges of separation or divorce.

When can divorce counseling help?Toggle answer

It can help when communication during separation is difficult, when co-parenting needs support, or when someone wants space to process the emotional side of divorce.

Is divorce counseling the same as couples counseling?Toggle answer

They're related but different — couples counseling generally aims to strengthen a relationship, while divorce counseling supports people through separating, sometimes including improving co-parenting communication.

What happens in a divorce counseling session?Toggle answer

Sessions typically focus on communication, emotional processing, and practical coping strategies relevant to the specific stage of separation or divorce.

Can divorce counseling help couples who are unsure about separating?Toggle answer

Yes — some couples use this kind of support to gain clarity, alongside or before deciding on next steps, rather than only after a decision has been made.
